omarchy 4.0.2-1
bar files from /usr/share/omarchy/shell/plugins/bar

The bar engine is still that 4.0.2 copy. The widget-hosting contract
(IslandPluginBarApi.qml, EntryShellProxy.qml, the facade and ownership code in
Bar.qml) is ported from omarchy 4.5.0-rc1
(/usr/share/omarchy/shell/plugins/bar/Bar.qml and shell/Ui/PluginBarApi.qml
at tag v4.5.0-rc1), adapted to island-bar's slot model and gated to 4.5
hosts only.

The 4.5 tray-section behaviour is ported the same way: widgets in the tray
layout section are hosted in the tray widget's drawer (Bar.qml's
traySectionComponent and the tray-host plumbing on ModuleSlot), the drawer
drop zone is BarModel.sectionDropZone, and centerBegin/centerEnd entries
join the center island where the stock bar's center box holds them.
